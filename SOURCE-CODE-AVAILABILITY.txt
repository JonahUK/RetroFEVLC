VideoLAN VLC
------------
Version: 3.0.24

Official source archive:
https://download.videolan.org/pub/videolan/vlc/3.0.24/vlc-3.0.24.tar.xz

Official SHA256:
e7cab503d1d7d5849b89d2cf0e1ee60d0ef6d012407791b644b9cfc0cc225fdf

Official VLC website:
https://www.videolan.org/

RetroFEVLC bundles libvlc.dll, libvlccore.dll and
326 VLC plugin DLLs.

These 328 DLLs were verified as byte-for-byte matches
to the official VLC 3.0.24 Windows x64 distribution.
