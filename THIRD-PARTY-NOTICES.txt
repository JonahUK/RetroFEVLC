VIDEOLAN VLC
------------
RetroFEVLC includes VLC 3.0.24 components supplied
by the VideoLAN project:

- libvlc.dll
- libvlccore.dll
- 326 VLC plugin DLLs

These binaries match VideoLAN's official Windows x64
VLC 3.0.24 distribution.

VLC GPL and LGPL licence texts and contributor
attributions are provided in the licenses directory.

VLC components and bundled codec dependencies may be
subject to different applicable licence terms.
